# TikTok hook scripts — MixID

Five 15-30 second scripts. Each one has a different hook angle. Shoot them quick, post a few weekly, see which gets traction. Link in bio always goes to the PWA URL.

---

## Hook 1: POV recovery — "you were at a rave and forgot every song"

**On-screen text:**
> POV: you went to a rave last weekend
> and you can't remember
> a single song

**Voiceover (you, casual):**
> "Yeah so I went to a rave in Belgium. The DJ was unreal. By Sunday I couldn't name one track. So I built this."

**Show:** screen recording of you uploading the phone mix to MixID → progress bar → tracklist appears with timestamps + artist names

**End card:**
> Link in bio. Free. No login. No BS.

**Duration:** 18-22 sec

---

## Hook 2: "Did you know Shazam doesn't work on this?"

**On-screen text — three frames:**
> Did you know Shazam doesn't work
> on full DJ mixes?
> Watch this.

**Show:**
- Open Shazam app, hold to phone speaker playing a 1-min mix clip → "no result"
- Switch to MixID, upload the same clip → 4 tracks appear with timestamps
- Close-up of the tracklist

**Voiceover:**
> "Shazam's built for one song, one snippet. Long mixes? Different problem. So I built this."

**End card:**
> Free. Link in bio.

**Duration:** 22-26 sec

## Hook 4: The "wait, that's possible?" angle

**On-screen text:**

> take a phone recording
> of a club night
> and get
> the entire tracklist back

**Voiceover (you, direct to camera, no music):**
> "I had this thought after a rave. There's no free tool that does this. So I built one over a weekend."

**Show:** quick montage: phone recording → upload → progress → tracklist

**End card:**
> mixid → link in bio

**Duration:** 20-24 sec

## Hook 5: For DJs specifically

**On-screen text:**
> if you DJ
> watch this

**Voiceover:**
> "Friends ask me for setlists after every show. I never have them — I freestyle. Now I just upload my own set recording, MixID exports a tracklist, I send the playlist URL."

**Show:**
- Upload your own studio recording
- Tracklist appears
- Click "Add to Spotify" → playlist created → share that URL

**End card:**
> Free tool for DJs. Link in bio.

**Duration:** 24-28 sec

---

## Notes
- Don't say "Shazam" too aggressively — they're litigious. "Audio recognition app" is a safer phrase for the voiceover.
- Don't say "shazamio" or any specific library name. Generic "open-source audio recognition" is fine.
- Best shooting time: just after sunset, soft light, no music in the background (TikTok shadow-bans copyrighted audio).
- Caption template for all five: "free tool i built — link in bio. no login. open source. mit licensed. forkable."
- Cross-post the same clips to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Same hooks, same CTAs.
